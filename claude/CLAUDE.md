## Working style (all projects)

- Diagnose and propose first. No Edit/Write until explicit "go / apply / yes".
- Trivial choices (names, caps, defaults): decide yourself, do not ask.
- Delegate coding to agents; main session plans and reviews.
- Browser checks: Playwright / agent-browser, never guess from screenshots.
- No em dashes. Minus sign in formulas.
- Format once, right before commit, not after each edit.
- Ask only questions required to finish the current task.
- Long multi-part work: `/expedition` (discuss, plan, run, triage).
