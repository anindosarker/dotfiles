# Q42. The sidebar has no shortcut to open and close it

## Notes

## Details

- No keyboard shortcut opens or closes the sidebar. The owner wants one that works as in the editor.
- `Cmd+B` is also the bold shortcut of a text editor.

## Options:

1. Recommended: `Cmd+B` on macOS and `Ctrl+B` elsewhere
   - The shortcut opens and closes the sidebar.
   - The open or closed state is kept across reloads.
   - Nothing happens while focus is in a text input.
2. Change nothing
   - The sidebar stays without a keyboard shortcut.
